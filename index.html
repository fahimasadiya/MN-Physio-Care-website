About Our Clinic

Welcome to MN PhysioCare Clinic

MN PhysioCare Clinic is a trusted Advanced Rehabilitation & Physiotherapy Centre dedicated to providing high-quality, evidence-based physiotherapy services. Our goal is to relieve pain, restore mobility, improve strength, and help every patient return to an active and healthy lifestyle.

Under the guidance of Dr. Mohsin Sheikh (PT), we provide personalized treatment plans using modern physiotherapy techniques and advanced rehabilitation methods for patients of all age groups.
