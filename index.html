About Us

MN PhysioCare – Advanced Rehabilitation & Physiotherapy Clinc

MN PhysioCare is a modern physiotherapy clinic dedicated to restoring health through movement and personalized care.

We specialize in treating Orthopedic, Neurological, Sports Injuries and Post-Surgical conditions using evidence-based rehabilitation techniques.
